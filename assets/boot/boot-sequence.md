# Asari-OS Boot Sequence

## Concept
Elegant, organic biotic awakening rather than aggressive digital glitch.

Inspired by:
- Asari natural biotics and nervous-system melding
- Scalp-crest geometry
- Soft mass-effect field aesthetics
- Transition into the hybrid Spectre Netrunner world

## Stages
1. Deep void / soft biotic pulse
2. Neural pathways + crest-inspired geometry forming and joining
3. Logo lock with "SYSTEM AWAKENING"
4. Status lines (Neural pathways synchronizing…)
5. Fade into desktop / rainy Night City backdrop

Keyframes are stored in this directory.
